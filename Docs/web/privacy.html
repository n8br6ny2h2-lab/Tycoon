Factory King – Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date]

1. Who we are

Factory King ("the game") is published by [company legal name], [address], [email] ("we").

2. Data stored on your device

Your game progress, settings and the identifiers of in-app purchases are stored on your device. If iCloud is enabled, a copy is kept in your own iCloud account so that your progress syncs between your devices. We cannot access this data.

3. Optional gameplay statistics

Only if you allow it (first-launch dialog or Settings), the game may record anonymous gameplay events such as "tutorial completed" or "factory unlocked". These statistics contain no name, email, contact details, location, advertising identifier or device identifier, and they are not used to track you across apps or websites. You can switch this off at any time in Settings. [If no analytics backend is active: the current version does not send these statistics anywhere.]

4. Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details. We keep only the transaction identifiers needed to deliver your purchases exactly once and to restore them.

5. Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, your achievements and leaderboard scores are handled by Apple according to Apple's privacy policy.

6. Advertising

The game shows no forced advertising. [If a rewarded-video network is added: optional videos are provided by [network], which may process device data according to its own policy: [link]. You can decline tracking through iOS's App Tracking Transparency prompt.]

7. Notifications

Reminder notifications are off by default and are only scheduled locally on your device if you enable them.

8. Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

9. Your rights and deleting data

"Settings › Reset progress" deletes your saved game on the device and in iCloud. For any other request about personal data under the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK, contact [email].

10. Changes

We will update this page if the way the game handles data changes.
