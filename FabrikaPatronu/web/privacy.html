Fabrika Patronu · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

In short

Fabrika Patronu does not collect, store or share any personal data. No account is needed. The game contains no ads, analytics or third-party tracking.

Data on your device

Your progress (company name, in-game money, stock, factories and settings) is kept only on your device, in the app's own storage. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it; you can also erase it any time by starting a Yeni oyun (New game) from the menu.

Internet

The game works fully offline and sends no data over the internet.

In-app purchases

This version has no in-app purchases and no ads. If they are added later, payments are handled entirely by Apple's App Store; we never see your payment details.

Children

The game does not collect personal data from anyone, of any age.
